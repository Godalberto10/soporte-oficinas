# The computer is slow or freezes

## Symptoms
- "My computer is slow", "the PC takes ages to open programs".
- The computer freezes for a few seconds or the mouse pointer jumps.
- No office service shows as degraded: it only happens on that computer.

## Cause
Too many programs open, the computer has not been restarted for days, or there
are pending updates. If it persists after restarting, it may be a lack of
memory or a failing disk.

## Steps
1. Ask the employee to save their work and restart the computer.
2. After restarting, close the programs and tabs they are not using.
3. If it becomes slow again, check the device record with `check_device`.
4. If nothing changes, contact the technical team: escalate to second level
   to check memory and disk.
